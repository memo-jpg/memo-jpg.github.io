When I entered my final year of University, I was especially hungry to prove myself.

I was looking for something to be a part of, it was then that I saw a flyer for an upcoming club on campus looking for members to join: Ars Vallis Ingenium (AVI) Robotics. And I thought to myself, "Huh... Robots are pretty cool."

Over the course of an academic year, we built, from the ground up, a working Underwater ROV, Rosie, and a Float, Adam.

I co-developed the first implemention of the movement control system, alongside my teammate, Chloe. I was placed in charge of making sure inputs were being correctly taken in from 2 Xbox controllers, for which I worked with the XInput C++ library. After that I had to find a way to send those inputs from our Window machine(s) to the Pi 4 we were running Linux on. Since we were using a tether, I figured to send that input data over networking packets for simplicity's sake.

The final code base continues to use a lot of the same logic and code that I had originally wrote and it was with my work and the hard work of my brilliant other club mates that we were able to place 4th in MATE ROV's 2026 World Championship Pioneer Division!
